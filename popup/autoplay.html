Let PocketBeat play music

Firefox stops add-ons and websites from playing sound on their own. PocketBeat plays music from a hidden page, so Firefox needs your OK once.

  1. In the address bar above, click the autoplay icon next to Extension (PocketBeat).
  2. Next to Autoplay, choose Allow Audio and Video.

Waiting for permission…

Done. Your music is playing again, and this tab will close.

Start a song in PocketBeat first, then open this page again.
